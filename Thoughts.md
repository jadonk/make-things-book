# Thoughts

The power of great thoughts is that they allow you to stop thinking. That's the magic of science; it allows us to stop thinking and start proving to ourselves that something is true. Of course, science doesn't *prove* anything; instead, it gives us personal experience with the behavior of things. This experience is the real gift of science, because it distracts us from what we cannot know, and, when practiced well, drives us to seek experience.
